The 05-01 tracer proved the drain mechanics on jobs; this plan gives the drain its other two per-pipeline branches.
Output: Loot + events rule packs, loot gates, loot FAQ reader, orchestrator branch dispatch, vendor watchlist, loot source wiring, and their tests. Plan 05-05 then layers the merger on top of the completed drain.
</objective>
